Narendra Modi Stadium

Location: GUJARAT

Facts: The stadium has 11 center pitches, allowing curators to prepare different types of wickets for various formats and conditions.

Facts: Unlike most international stadiums that have two dressing rooms, Narendra Modi Stadium has four, making it easier to host back-to-back matches and tournaments.

Facts: The stadium is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and technology, ensuring a world-class experience for players and spectators alike.

Wankhede Stadium

Location: MUMBAI, MAHARASHTRA

Facts: The most famous moment in the stadium's history is when MS Dhoni hit the winning six against Sri Lanka to win the 2011 Cricket World Cup for India.

Facts: The stadium has a unique feature called the "Wankhede Wall," which is a high boundary on one side of the ground that can make it challenging for batsmen to clear.

Facts: The stadium is known for its passionate fans and electric atmosphere, especially during high-stakes matches.

Eden Gardens

Location: kolkata

Facts: Eden Gardens was established in 1864, making it the oldest cricket stadium in India and one of the oldest in the world.

Facts: The stadium is known for its vibrant atmosphere and has hosted numerous iconic matches throughout its history.

Facts: The stadium has witnessed some of the most memorable moments in cricket history, including the 2001 Test match between India and Australia, where VVS Laxman and Rahul Dravid's partnership turned the game around for India.

dharmshala Stadium

Location: chandigarh

Facts: The stadium is surrounded by the snow-capped Dhauladhar Range, giving spectators breathtaking views during matches.

Facts: The stadium is known for its unique architecture and has hosted numerous international cricket matches.

lords cricket Stadium

Location: england

Facts: Lord's Cricket Ground is known as the "Home of Cricket" and has hosted numerous historic matches.

Facts: The stadium features the iconic Lord's Pavilion, which is a symbol of cricketing heritage and tradition.

chinna swamy Stadium

Location: banglore

Facts: The stadium is named after the legendary Indian cricketer C. N. Annadurai, also known as Chinna Swamy.

Facts: The stadium has a seating capacity of around 40,000 and is known for its passionate cricket fans who create an electrifying atmosphere during matches.

newlands cricket ground

Location: new zealand

Facts: Newlands Cricket Ground is a historic venue in Cape Town, South Africa, known for its unique setting and rich cricketing heritage.

Facts: The stadium is situated at the foot of Table Mountain, providing stunning views for spectators and players alike.

melbourne Stadium

Location: austrilia

Facts: Melbourne Cricket Ground is one of the most iconic cricket venues in the world, known for its rich history and thrilling matches.

Facts: The stadium has a seating capacity of over 100,000, making it one of the largest cricket stadiums globally and creating an electrifying atmosphere during matches.

Facts: The MCG has hosted numerous historic moments in cricket, including the first-ever Test match in 1877 and the 1992 Cricket World Cup final where Pakistan defeated England.

queens park oval

Location: Famous cricket venue.

Facts: Queens Park Oval is a historic cricket ground in Sydney, Australia, known for its unique atmosphere and rich cricketing heritage.

Facts: The stadium has hosted numerous memorable matches, including the famous 1981 Ashes Test where Ian Botham's heroics turned the game in England's favor.
